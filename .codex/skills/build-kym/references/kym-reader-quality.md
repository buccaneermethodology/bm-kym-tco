# KYM Reader Quality And Execution Projection

Use for Goal, Loop Goal, Stage Plan, or governance-heavy input after ledger and before validation. Improve reader usefulness without relaxing provenance or candidate-only boundaries.

## Time and authority

Classify status as **source snapshot** (ingested file statement), **verified current fact** (separate current evidence), or **candidate/proposal** (requested future state). With one Goal file, say source-only and do not call it current.

## Node pass

- **Customer** answers who benefits and why; Builder/reviewer/source speaker is not automatically a Product actor.
- **Info** states product/experiment and source boundary. **Naming** defines terms only. **Scope/Exclusions** hold maximum claim and non-authorizations.
- **Business Rules** group required/fail-closed/separated behavior under a semantic parent; preserve constraints. **Documents/Problems/Standards** hold materials, questions, protocols.
- **Deliverables** concrete outputs; **Tools** named tools; **Schedule** order/dependency/pause-resume; **Team** execution, authority, review, independence. Leave source-silent nodes empty.
- **Target Profiles** answer enduring user/product value. A Goal or trial is evidence for a Target, not automatically the Target itself.

For controlled experiments, keep effect status, functional/safety/claim acceptance, and final verdict separate. Preserve mode, approval, no-write, non-promotion; never generalize one bounded result into generic, semantic, production, or approval authority.

Before render, ensure populated nodes answer their question with source-supported text, key Product branches have plain language, verification gaps remain explicit, and Target candidate/approval boundary is visible.
